Underwriting Variables Not Accounted For

Actual residential mortgage underwriting in the United States requires the highly rigorous, deeply invasive verification of highly complex, individual variables that cannot possibly be captured by an online calculator. These massive variables include, but are absolutely not limited to, your exact FICO credit profile history, fully verified W-2 or 1099 gross income, exact property location tax assessments, aggressive dynamic homeowners insurance premiums based on specific zip codes, and your absolute total Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio. If you possess a high DTI or a low credit score, a private lender will completely reject the interest rate estimated by this calculator and force you into a vastly higher, more expensive pricing tier via Loan Level Price Adjustments (LLPAs).

Dynamic Federal Guidelines

Lending guidelines actively established by the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA), the Department of Housing and Urban Development (FHA), the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), and the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) are highly dynamic and subject to immediate, drastic modification by federal legislative action entirely without notice. The VA Funding Fee, FHA MIP percentages, and IRS SALT caps represented in our software are based on currently published data but may suddenly shift.

Users of this digital platform must absolutely consult with a state-licensed, federally registered mortgage loan originator, a Certified Public Accountant (CPA), or a verified Certified Financial Planner (CFP) prior to executing any massive, legally binding real estate purchase contract or initiating a massive debt restructuring or refinancing agreement.
